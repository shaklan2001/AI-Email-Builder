The current AI experience generates workflows too early.

We want the AI to behave like a sales campaign strategist rather than a workflow generator.

## Problem

Current behavior:

User provides an initial campaign description.

AI immediately generates a workflow and emails.

This creates confusion because users do not understand:

- what information AI extracted
- what information is still missing
- what they should do next

The experience feels abrupt.

## Desired Experience

The AI should guide the user through campaign creation.

Conversation flow:

User Prompt

↓

AI Confirms Understanding

↓

AI Shows Collected Information

↓

AI Shows Missing Information

↓

AI Asks One Follow-Up Question

↓

Repeat Until Complete

↓

Generate Workflow

↓

Generate Emails

↓

Review

↓

Activate

## Required Campaign Information

Before workflow generation, collect:

Required:

- Product / Service
- Business Goal
- Target Audience
- Tone
- CTA

Optional:

- Images
- Attachments
- Landing Page URL
- Company Information
- USP
- Competitor Information

Workflow generation must not occur until all required fields are collected.

## AI Response Format

After every user message:

1. Confirm what was understood.

Example:

✓ Product: AirPure Pro

✓ Goal: Book Product Demos

✓ Audience: Homeowners

2. Show progress.

Example:

Campaign Setup Progress

██████░░░░

60% Complete

3. Ask only ONE follow-up question.

Example:

What tone would you like the emails to use?

- Professional
- Friendly
- Casual
- Luxury

Do not ask multiple questions in a single message.

## Completion Behavior

When all required information is collected:

AI should say:

"Perfect. I now have enough information to build your campaign."

Then:

1. Generate workflow
2. Generate emails
3. Present review screen

## Workflow Generation UX

After generation:

Show:

Workflow Summary

Example:

Product Launch Email

↓

Wait 3 Days

↓

Reply?

├─ Yes → Demo Scheduling Email

└─ No → Follow-Up Email

Also show:

Generated Assets

- Workflow
- Email Templates
- Recipient Strategy

Then ask:

What would you like to do next?

- Review Emails
- Modify Workflow
- Activate Campaign

## Check When Done

- Workflow is not generated prematurely
- AI confirms extracted information
- AI asks one question at a time
- Progress indicator shown
- Workflow generated only after required fields collected
- User always understands the next action
- Conversation feels guided and professional
